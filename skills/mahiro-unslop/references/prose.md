# Prose lane

Read the complete target, including headings and ending, before searching for
patterns. Repair a contextual defect only when the case for changing it is
clearer than preservation. Punctuation, cadence, vocabulary and structure alone
prove neither a defect nor AI authorship.

## Diagnose compactly

Quote the smallest defective span and explain its effect here. Useful probes:

- Empty participial framing (“highlighting”, “ensuring”) with no actual mechanism;
  vague attribution presented as evidence; praise that never says what changed.
- Inflated words or indirect verbs where a plain equivalent preserves meaning.
- Repeated “not just X, but Y”, forced threes, false ranges and synonym cycling
  that obscure an otherwise direct point.
- Decorative bold, repetitive inline labels, unnecessary headings or punctuation
  that interrupt reading rather than clarify it.
- Chatbot greetings, flattery, filler, excessive hedging and generic recap endings.
- Abstract metaphors, personified code or mannered fragments that hide the actor,
  mechanism, action or result.
- Dense sentences, weak verbs propped up by adverbs, or over-compressed fragments
  that make readers reconstruct missing relationships.

These are prompts, not a taboo-word catalog. Protect literal and domain-valid
terms, quotations, attributions, genre-natural rhetoric and deliberate voice.
An em dash, passive voice, curly quote or title-case heading is not itself a
finding. Do not impose an English punctuation style on another language.

## Preservation-first repair

For audit-only, report findings and relevant protections without rewriting.
For authorized cleanup, edit only sentences with confirmed findings, making the
smallest repair. Copy every other sentence byte-for-byte in its original order
and paragraph. With no findings, return the source exactly.

Preserve facts, quantities, dates, names, quotations, citations, code, units,
scope, uncertainty, attribution, register and meaning. Preserve force-bearing
“never”, “must” and “all” in safety, security, legal and technical rules. Do not
add claims, anecdotes, advice, confidence, personality or a new conclusion.
Do not turn flowing writing into staccato anti-slop prose.

A missing citation alone is not permission to delete a claim or invent a source.
Keep attributed claims intact; flag internal conflicts separately. If nearby
prose already explains the uncertainty, do not redundantly qualify it. External
fact-checking is a separate requested scope; cleanup must not silently change
truth based on remembered knowledge.

## Check

Compare original and result sentence by sentence. Account for every changed
span; re-read negations, conditions, quantities, party relationships and quoted
material. Confirm that each deletion removes no meaning and each simplification
preserves force. No upstream scanners, scoring system or preset is required.
Report confirmed repairs, intentional protections and unresolved ambiguity.

Example: “In order to export the report, select Export” can become “To export the
report, select Export”. “The job must never retry after 3 failures” keeps its
limit and force exactly. If already clear, leave it alone.
