## Upstream principles retained as bounded tools

- ground characters in product context before generation
- severe shape, color, line, and detail budgets
- intentional non-default composition; lower-corner emergence and paired
  left/right draws remain available techniques, not universal defaults
- omit commercial end-use words from raw image prompts
- one independent image per call, exact receipts, no hidden retry or repair
- human visual selection before interpretation or production work

## Mahiro-owned extension

The upstream workflow ends at independent prompt-only character candidates and
avoids previous candidates as references. This skill adds a post-selection route:

1. freeze one human-selected raw image as identity authority
2. pass that exact raw directly to every reference/edit call
3. vary one approved structural axis while keeping other identity properties
   fixed
4. compare requested versus observed output under the target crop or mask
5. hand the selected adaptation to production without regenerating it

Both routes live inside one skill, but the human gate separates them. This keeps
the method self-contained without pretending creation and identity-preserving
adaptation are the same evidence problem.

## Care evidence — 2026-09-06 to 2026-09-07

### Explore evidence

A controlled blind Care/GPT Image 2 proof compared the pinned upstream workflow
against an equally resourced unassisted control: identical frozen brief, six
one-image calls per condition, fresh sessions, no references, zero retries or
repairs, and raw outputs shown before reveal.

Mahiro selected the upstream-guided batch and ranked **X3 > X1 > X4**. X3 and X1
were independent Sprout-companion draws from opposite lower corners. This is
positive bounded evidence for simple character/IP exploration on that product,
model, judge, and run—not universal superiority or production readiness.

### Adapt evidence

A later prompt-only attempt produced a cute white rounded companion with pink
cheeks hugging a mint sprout, but Mahiro rejected its centered mascot portrait.

Four GPT Image 2 reference/edit calls then used that raw character as the sole
image reference while changing corner composition. Every first return was
preserved with no retry or post-processing. Mahiro selected **C1 lower-left**
because it retained the already-generated character while moving it into the
intended composition.

The upper-corner outputs provided useful negative evidence: an upright body with
paired ears still occupied the lower mass and pushed an ear into the approximate
iOS mask. Assigned prompt labels therefore cannot prove observed compliance.

## mahiro-skills Explore evidence — 2026-09-07

A later six-call GPT Image 2 Explore proof used three product-grounded directions
with independent left/right draws, no references, and zero retries or
post-processing. Mahiro's human verdict was **PASS**. Within that batch, the
left-side candidate followed each direction more closely than its right-side
pair.

The transferable correction is not “always generate left.” When an accepted
candidate only needs an exact opposite-side mirror and all directional cues may
reverse, a deterministic flip preserves identity and should short-circuit a new
provider call. Repositioning without reversed handedness, gesture, gaze, marks,
or lighting remains a reference-edit job. The same proof also corrected corner
placement from a default into one direction-owned composition option.

## Evidence boundary

- One Care result does not prove cross-product or cross-provider reliability.
- The mahiro-skills sample does not establish that left-side generation is
  universally better; it supports avoiding unnecessary generation for a
  mirror-only derivative.
- Reference/edit preserved enough identity for Mahiro's judgment; it does not
  guarantee pixel-level identity or eliminate stochastic drift.
- This evidence concerns character/IP creation and composition adaptation, not
  broad abstract marks, typography, final vectors, legal clearance, or runtime
  integration.
- A future material workflow change requires a fresh human-reviewed proof, not
  merely a mechanically successful generation or relative model ranking.
