## Key Changes and Decisions

- Explore creates independent candidates only before a human identity lock.
- Adapt receives the same exact selected source for every model-generated
  candidate and never chains generated outputs.
- Composition is owned by subject, product meaning, silhouette, and target
  surface—not by a universal centered or corner template.
- A pure mirror request first checks text, symbols, marks, handed props, gesture,
  gaze, pose, lighting, and target-side semantics. If every cue may reverse, use
  a deterministic flip, preserve hashes, record zero provider calls, and label
  the result a derivative rather than a generated candidate.
- Repositioning that must preserve orientation remains a direct-reference Adapt
  job.
- Historical Care corner evidence remains dated provenance rather than current
  default doctrine.

## AI Diary

I began with a technically plausible but too literal reading of the upstream
workflow. The source had positive evidence for lower-left and lower-right
compositions, so I promoted that into the first version as if it were a default.
Mahiro's correction exposed the mistake cleanly: I had replaced one habitual
template—centered mascots—with another template—corner mascots. The better rule
is not about coordinates at all; composition should belong to the character,
product meaning, silhouette, and target surface. I am glad the real six-image
proof happened before release because the visual evidence made this obvious in a
way that source-only review did not.

The second useful correction was even more operational. I treated paired
left/right outputs as requiring independent image-generation calls, although an
exact mirror may be a deterministic image transform. That added stochastic
identity drift and unnecessary spend to a case where byte-grounded geometry is
better. The resulting mirror audit is stronger because it does not blindly flip:
it checks text, handedness, props, gaze, marks, and lighting first. I also had two
avoidable shell-command typos while reading files and one stale assertion after
changing the Adapt introduction. Both were caught before mutation or release,
but they were signs that I was moving too quickly after a long session. The final
state is substantially better because Mahiro challenged the workflow at the
decision level rather than merely choosing an image.

## Honest Feedback

First, I overfit bounded evidence. The upstream Care result showed that a corner
composition could beat a centered portrait, but it did not establish corner
placement as universal doctrine. Second, I made the imagegen proof more expensive
and slower than necessary by treating every left/right pair as independently
generative. A mirror-safe short-circuit should have been considered before the
call plan. Third, my closeout language initially framed the six outputs as not
meeting the benchmark even though the procedural and visual result was good
enough for Mahiro's intended skill test. That was an ownership error: I could
report excess 3D depth and weaker metaphors mechanically, but the overall taste
verdict belonged to Mahiro. Finally, two malformed shell reads and a stale test
expectation were small but preventable execution mistakes. They did not alter
files or provider state, yet they lengthened a session that already had a strong
proof. The improvement is concrete: use exact source owners, smaller read
commands, deterministic transforms before stochastic calls, and narrower
language around human visual acceptance.

## Lessons Learned

- Bounded visual evidence supports a technique, not a universal placement rule.
- For an exact mirror, deterministic identity preservation is preferable to
  stochastic regeneration when every directional cue may reverse.
- Provider receipt integrity includes proving zero calls before recovering from a
  controller failure.
- Mechanical critique and human taste acceptance are different verdicts.

These lessons were already promoted into the canonical skill and persistent
Mahiro preference memory during the session. No duplicate non-canonical learning
file is warranted.
